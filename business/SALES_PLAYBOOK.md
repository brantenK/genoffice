# Zanostack (GenOffice) — Commercial Sales Playbook

**Target Audience**: Sales Representatives, Founders, Account Executives, and Channel Partners  
**Objective**: Convert SME Contractors, Consultancies, and Professional Firms from Disconnected SaaS into Paid Zanostack Customers.

---

## 1. Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

### Tier 1 Beachhead: Government & Corporate Contractors
* **Industry**: Construction, Civil Engineering, IT Services, Security, Logistics, Medical Supplies.
* **Company Size**: 3 to 75 employees.
* **Key Pain**: Submitting bids on public tenders (e.g., e-Tender portal, municipal RFPs). Constantly terrified of administrative disqualification due to missing SARS tax clearance, expired B-BBEE certificates, or certified ID copies older than 3 months.
* **Economic Buyer**: Managing Director, Head of Estimations, Operations Director.

### Tier 2: Privacy-Sensitive Professional Services
* **Industry**: Boutique Law Firms, Accounting Practices, Financial Advisors, Engineering Consultancies.
* **Company Size**: 2 to 30 employees.
* **Key Pain**: High SaaS bills (Google Workspace + HubSpot + DocuSign) and strict compliance obligations regarding client confidentiality.
* **Economic Buyer**: Managing Partner, Finance Director, Practice Manager.

## 5. Objection Handling Guide

### Objection 1: *"We already pay for Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace."*
* **Response**: *"That's completely normal! Many of our clients started with Microsoft 365. But Microsoft 365 doesn't shred your tender documents, doesn't verify statutory compliance returnables, doesn't have an integrated pipeline CRM, and doesn't track 90-day police stamps. Most companies adopt Zanostack specifically for their bidding and operations teams, and end up saving money by downgrading their expensive Microsoft enterprise licenses."*

### Objection 2: *"Why desktop instead of a cloud web app?"*
* **Response**: *"Two major reasons: Security and Speed. When you're dealing with competitive pricing, government tenders, and company financials, you don't want your data sitting in a multi-tenant cloud where foreign servers or competitors could access it. Zanostack is 100% local-first: your documents stay on your machine, it opens instantly, and it works seamlessly even when you're on a job site with zero internet connection."*

### Objection 3: *"Is my data safe and compliant with privacy laws (POPIA / GDPR)?"*
* **Response**: *"Zanostack is fundamentally more compliant than any cloud SaaS tool. Because our database and document storage live entirely on your computer's encrypted local drive, you achieve zero-data-leakage compliance by design. No client records or bank statements ever touch our servers."*
