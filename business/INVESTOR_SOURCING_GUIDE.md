## 3. Angel Investor Networks & Syndicates

Angels write smaller, faster cheques ($10,000 to $50,000) and can help you close your round before institutions commit.

* **AngelList / Wellfound** ([wellfound.com](https://wellfound.com) & [angellist.com](https://www.angellist.com)): Search for syndicates and individual angels who tag *"B2B SaaS"*, *"Productivity"*, *"Future of Work"*, or *"Procurement Tech"*.
* **Jozi Angels** ([joziangels.co.za](https://www.joziangels.co.za)): Premier angel investor network in Johannesburg backing early-stage tech businesses with viable commercial traction.
* **African Business Angel Network (ABAN)** ([abanangels.org](https://abanangels.org)): Umbrella network connecting angel groups across Africa and the diaspora.
* **UK Business Angels Association (UKBAA)** ([ukbaa.org.uk](https://ukbaa.org.uk)): Largest network for UK and Commonwealth angel syndicates.

## 4. Free Investor Directories & Direct Cold Outreach

You do not need an introduction to pitch many of the best investors today. Use these open directories:

1. **OpenVC** ([openvc.app](https://www.openvc.app)):
   - A 100% free, searchable directory of over 1,000+ venture funds.
   - Filter by: **Stage** (Pre-Seed / Seed), **Sector** (B2B SaaS / Productivity / Enterprise), and **Target Geography**.
   - Many funds on OpenVC accept direct pitch deck uploads without warm introductions.
2. **Signal by NFX** ([signal.nfx.com](https://signal.nfx.com)):
   - The definitive database of tech investors.
   - Search by thesis: *"Future of Work"*, *"B2B Software"*, or *"Desktop & Local-First"*.
   - View which investors are currently actively writing cheques.
3. **LinkedIn Advanced Search**:
   - Search for: `"Partner" OR "Principal" OR "Angel Investor" + "B2B SaaS"`.
   - Send the direct pitch script provided in `business/SALES_PLAYBOOK.md`.
